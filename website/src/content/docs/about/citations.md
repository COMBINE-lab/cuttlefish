---
title: Citations
description: How to cite Cuttlefish 1, Cuttlefish 2, and Cuttlefish 3.
---

If you use Cuttlefish in your work, please include the citations appropriate to
the version you ran.

## Cuttlefish (original)

[10.1093/bioinformatics/btab309](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btab309)

> Jamshed Khan, Rob Patro. Cuttlefish: fast, parallel and low-memory compaction
> of de Bruijn graphs from large-scale genome collections. *Bioinformatics*,
> Volume 37, Issue Supplement_1, July 2021, Pages i177–i186.

## Cuttlefish 2

[10.1186/s13059-022-02743-6](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-022-02743-6)

> Khan, J., Kokot, M., Deorowicz, S. et al. Scalable, ultra-fast, and low-memory
> construction of compacted de Bruijn graphs with Cuttlefish 2. *Genome Biology*
> 23, 190 (2022).

## Cuttlefish 3

Cuttlefish 3 appears as a proceedings paper at **RECOMB 2026** (*Research in
Computational Molecular Biology*). A preprint is on bioRxiv:
[10.1101/2025.02.02.636161](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.02.02.636161)

> Jamshed Khan, Laxman Dhulipala, and Rob Patro. Fast and Scalable Parallel
> External-Memory Construction of Colored Compacted de Bruijn Graphs with
> Cuttlefish 3. *Proceedings of RECOMB 2026*; preprint: *bioRxiv* (2025).
